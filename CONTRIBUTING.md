# Contribution Guidelines / Katkı Rehberi

Thanks for helping make this list better! / Listeyi geliştirdiğin için teşekkürler!

## Adding an item

- Search previous suggestions before making a new one to avoid duplicates.
- One pull request per suggestion.
- Put the link in the most relevant section, at the bottom of that section.
- Use this format: `- [Name](https://link) - Short description ending with a period.`
- Keep descriptions short and neutral. No marketing language.
- The resource must be useful for developers in Türkiye or about Türkiye/Turkish.
- Projects must be maintained, documented and publicly accessible. Links must work.
- Check your spelling and grammar. English descriptions are preferred; Turkish names are fine.

## Not accepted

- Dead links, paywalled-only content, or affiliate links.
- Personal projects without documentation or a clear use.
- Pirated content or tools that violate laws or terms of service.

## Updating or removing an item

If a link is dead, outdated or no longer maintained, open a pull request or issue explaining why.

By contributing, you agree that your contributions are released under [CC0](LICENSE).
